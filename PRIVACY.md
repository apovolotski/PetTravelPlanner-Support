# Pet Travel Planner — Android Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Pet Travel Planner is provided by **Alexander Povolotskiy**. This policy covers the Android app with package name `com.apovolotski.pettravelplanner`.

## Information kept on your device

Pet profiles, species, breed, age, weight, vaccination records, saved trips, dates, checklists and notes are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. These records are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. The app has no accounts, advertising or analytics SDKs. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are excluded for app records.

## Hotel searches and maps

Hotel searches and maps require internet access. When you search a destination, the text is sent to your device’s Android geocoding provider. Hotel searches send the searched coordinates to OpenStreetMap Overpass services, using `overpass-api.de` with `overpass.kumi.systems` as a fallback. Viewing a map requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap using bundled Leaflet code.

These providers receive information needed to answer the request, along with network information such as your IP address. They process it under their own policies. The developer does not control their logs or retention. The app does not save a location-search history. Provider processing is not represented as anonymous or ephemeral.

## Optional location

The app requests approximate foreground location only when you choose **Near me**. It uses a single location fix to search nearby hotels and sends the resulting searched coordinates to the hotel-search provider. It does not request precise or background location, continuously track you, or save a location history.

You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings and search by destination instead. Pets, airline references and saved-trip planning do not require location permission.

## Sharing and external services

Trip sharing opens Android’s sharing interface only when you choose **Share trip checklist**. The displayed trip details, pet name, checklist and notes are included. You choose the receiving app or recipient.

Airline and government links, hotel websites, external maps and the dialer open only when you select them. The app does not place a phone call automatically. External apps and websites handle information under their own policies.

## Retention and deletion

You can delete individual pet profiles and saved trips within the app. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app removes its local records. The app does not offer cloud synchronization or cross-device recovery. Copies you intentionally share are controlled by their recipients and destinations.

Third-party search and map providers control their own request logs and retention; deleting local app records does not delete those third-party logs.

## Security

Network requests made by the app’s HTTP client and map use HTTPS. Local records rely on Android’s application sandbox and your device’s security. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Support correspondence is handled through the developer’s email provider. Avoid including unnecessary personal information.
