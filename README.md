## Android closed test

Pet Travel Planner helps adults plan travel with pets: save pet profiles and vaccination notes, browse airline policies, check country requirements, plan trips and find hotels.

1. [Join the Pet Travel Planner Android Testers group](https://groups.google.com/g/pet-travel-planner-android-testers) with your Google account. Use **Join group**; the member list and conversations are restricted to the owner.
2. Using the **same Google account**, [enroll in the Google Play closed test](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.apovolotski.pettravelplanner), then follow the Google Play installation link.
3. Try Pets, Airlines, Planner/country rules and Hotels. Send feedback to [apovolotski@gmail.com](mailto:apovolotski@gmail.com), including your Android version and any steps that reproduce a problem. Please avoid sharing private pet, travel or location information.

**Status, 5 November 2026:** Android version 1.0.1 has been submitted for closed-testing review. Google checks and approval are pending. The enrollment or download page may be unavailable until Google approves and activates this release. This is a test version; hotel searches depend on external services and may time out.

Google requires at least **12 testers enrolled in the Play closed test continuously for 14 days** before the developer can apply for production access. Joining the group alone does not count as enrolling in the Play test. Please remain enrolled and use the app during the test period.

Country requirements and airline policies can change. Confirm current rules with the relevant authority and airline before travel. Hotel listings are not a guarantee of pet acceptance; contact the property directly.

This repository contains public policy and support documents. The application source is maintained separately.
